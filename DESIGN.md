# Jobfly visual system

A light, restrained interface: white panels on a faint violet neutral, dark ink,
violet primary actions, green positive feedback. DM Sans for controls and data;
Instrument Serif only for the main title. Fine borders, spacious onboarding,
a large Three.js world and a readable neural panel. No cutesy labels or invented
product vocabulary. Mobile setup comes before the explanation. Support keyboard
controls, reduced motion, text-based job selection, upload review, errors, and retry.
