# Jobfly

## Register
product

## Platform
web

## Users
Job seekers and the JSON Resume community exploring a fly-brain job search.

## Product Purpose
Start with a resume, get real jobs, and steer future exploration through feedback on fly landings.

## Design Principles
Light mode. Plain, confident copy. No fictional jobs. Resume review comes before a new session. Each session has a persistent, private-by-possession URL. Uploads convert to editable JSON Resume. The full brain and Three.js visualizations remain central.
